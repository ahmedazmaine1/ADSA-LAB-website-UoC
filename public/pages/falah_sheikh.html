Falah Sheikh

B.Sc. Student · DANSA Lab

  • Affiliation: Department of Computer Science, University of Calgary
  • Research: Machine Learning, Artificial Intelligence, Data-Driven Methods, Scientific Computing
  • ORCID: 0009-0003-8770-6806
  • Website: falahsheikh.com

Falah Sheikh is a researcher working at the intersection of machine learning, AI, and human-centered computing. His research examines why machine learning systems succeed or fail, and how to design interpretable tools that support decision-making in high-stakes settings.

He began his research career in computational astronomy, developing deep learning methods for dating lunar surfaces from visual imagery. His subsequent work in medical AI focused on lightweight, explainable models for early Alzheimer's detection from MRI, with an emphasis on the kind of interpretability clinicians can actually use. More recently, he has been studying hallucination behavior in large language models, examining how subtle shifts in prompt phrasing affect model reliability.

Falah is a recipient of the Alberta Innovates Summer Research Studentship and the Community Mental Health and Well-Being Strategy Award. Outside of research, he writes about science and technology, makes art, and builds tools that grow out of his work.
