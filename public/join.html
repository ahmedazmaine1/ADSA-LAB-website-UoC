● Opportunities

Join the Lab

We’re looking for curious people who want to work at the intersection of data, networks, and real-world impact.

Our work spans data mining, social network analysis, data privacy, bioinformatics, and machine learning. Prospective graduate students (PhD and MSc), undergraduate (BSc) researchers, and external collaborators are all welcome.

To apply, reach out to Prof. Reda Alhajj and ask whether he is currently taking on new students. Include a short note about your research interests, your CV, and — for prospective students — your transcripts.
