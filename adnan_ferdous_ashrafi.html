Adnan Ferdous Ashrafi received the B.Sc. and M.Sc. degrees in Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) from the Islamic University of Technology (IUT), and he is currently pursuing a M.Sc. degree in Computer Science at the University of Calgary under the Advanced Database Systems and Applications (ADSA) Lab. From 2015 to 2025, he was a Senior Lecturer at Stamford University Bangladesh, where he dedicated himself to fostering the next generation of computer scientists. He has also served as a trainer for the software design and architecture course with BASIS, bridging the gap between theory and practical application in software development.

Adnan has made significant contributions to the academic community, publishing several high-impact research works in reputable journals, including IEEE, Elsevier, and Springer. His research interests span bioinformatics, biomedical image processing, image segmentation, computer vision, and data mining.
